# Parts list

Prices are approximate UK high-street / online prices, October 2026. Everything
is available from a hobby shop, a maker supplier, or a certain very large
marketplace. Nothing here is specialist except the ball bearings and the wire.

## Tools

Scissors, hobby knife (**adult**), wire strippers (**adult**), hot glue gun
(**adult**), pencil, ruler, timer or phone stopwatch for the tilt test.

## Things you do NOT need

- **No mains power, no plugs, no soldering iron.** Everything runs on AAs.
- No 3D printer, no laser cutter, no special tools.
- No exact measurements that matter: the board size and tilt are a starting
  point, and the "test before you glue" rule in the build guide is what makes it
  work.
