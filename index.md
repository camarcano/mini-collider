# The Colliderscope 🎡⚛️

A tiny model of a particle collider, made for STEM week by three Year 4
children and one grown-up: a physical machine you can fire, a presentation
three children can give, and a live simulation.

Everything on this site works **offline** too — download any file and it just
runs. No installs, no internet, no accounts.

## If you only have one evening

Print the [board template](build/layout_template.html), get a 3 m length of
16 mm clear tubing, two 14 mm steel balls, a board, a bag of beads and a
buzzer-and-battery kit. Build the two tracks and the cup, launch the balls with
your fingers, and open the [simulation](simulation/) on a laptop. That is a
working demonstration for about &pound;20 — the electromagnets are the part
worth coming back for.
